## 📊 Project Overview

This project applies **Business Intelligence (BI)** principles and **Microsoft Power BI** to the Superstore sales dataset. The objective is to transform business data into actionable insights that support evidence-based decision-making.

The analysis evaluates sales, profitability, products, customer segments, regions and trends over time, while identifying business risks, opportunities and management actions.

## 🎯 Business Objective

The primary objective is to evaluate sales and profitability performance and generate insights that can support better business decisions.

The analysis addresses:

- Sales and profit trends over time
- Product, category and sub-category performance
- Profitability drivers
- Customer segment contribution
- Regional and state-level performance
- Loss-making areas and business risks
- Opportunities for improving profitability and sustainable growth

## ❓ Key Business Questions

1. How have sales and profit changed over time?
2. Which products and categories generate the highest sales?
3. Which products and sub-categories are most profitable?
4. Which areas are generating losses?
5. Which customer segment contributes the most sales?
6. Which regions and states perform best?
7. What factors influence profit margin?
8. What actions can management take to improve profitability?

## 💡 Key Insights

- Total sales were approximately **$2.30M**, with total profit of approximately **$286.40K**.
- Sales and profit increased consistently from **2014 to 2017**, indicating positive overall business growth.
- **Technology** generated the highest category sales at approximately **$0.84M**.
- The **West** region recorded the highest sales at approximately **$0.73M**.
- **Copiers** generated the highest sub-category profit at approximately **$56K**.
- **Tables and Bookcases** recorded negative profitability and require further investigation.
- The **Consumer** segment contributed approximately **50.6% of total sales**.
- Key Influencers analysis identified **Paper** as an important factor associated with higher profit margin relative to the baseline.

## 🚨 Business Risks & Opportunities

### Risks

- Loss-making sub-categories can reduce overall profitability.
- High sales volume does not always translate into proportional profit.
- Revenue/sales concentration in the Consumer segment may create growth opportunities in other segments.

### Opportunities

- Expand high-margin products and categories.
- Improve performance in weaker regions.
- Grow Corporate and Home Office customer segments.
- Review pricing, discounts and costs for loss-making products.

## ✅ Recommendations

1. Prioritize high-performing regions while improving weaker markets.
2. Review pricing, discounts and operating costs for loss-making sub-categories.
3. Increase focus on high-margin and profitable products.
4. Develop targeted strategies to grow Corporate and Home Office segments.
5. Monitor **Sales + Profit + Profit Margin** together when evaluating performance.
